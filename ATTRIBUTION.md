# Attribution and source boundaries

The original sprite study and interface components were developed for Mad Money Tycoon. No general reuse licence has been assigned to the project. Do not infer one from public repository access.

- **Geist / Geist Mono:** Vercel, distributed under SIL Open Font License 1.1; licence text is retained with the fonts.
- **Office photography:** Matt Fry’s [65 Porter](https://mattfryed.com/65porter) and [266 Johnson](https://mattfryed.com/266-johnson) portfolio records. [PSF Projects](https://www.psfprojects.com/workplace/madwell-creative-agency) credits Zach Pontz for its Porter gallery. Individual attribution and file hashes are in `Design/assets/office-references/manifest.json`. Unestablished credits are marked as such.
- **Boerum street reference:** supplied screenshot of Google street imagery; capture date unknown. It supplies a facade reference, not an authenticated identity or measured plan.
- **The Zero Machine:** supplied 58-page reconstruction dated September 2, 2026. Preserved as an attributed source; its allegations and conclusions are not independent findings by this project.
- **IRS Form 941-X:** April 2024 form, retained as an interface-layout reference. The separate T-01 specimen is fictional and is not an IRS form.
- **NYC North Brooklyn report:** source for the described office context; printed p.66 / PDF p.86. Page-level limits are retained in the office research.
- **RCT, Poolside.fm and other supplied imagery:** visual and interaction references only. Unknown image authors are not replaced with guessed credits. Third-party game graphics do not enter the original sprite catalogue.
- **Unused mod files:** retained in `Design/archive/quarantine/third-party-mod/` as supplied reference material. They are not used by the project or offered as an original game asset pack.

The external financial validation collection remains outside this repository. The published inventory identifies inspected exports by relative source locator and hash; it does not pretend those files are present or newly authenticated.

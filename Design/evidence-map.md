## Non-negotiable distinctions in the model

1. **1072 is not a bank.** It is a synthetic clearing account. A spatial service building may provide access to payment work; it must not store the modeled $141.578m field as cash.
2. **An economic episode has several representations.** A bill, platform Paid object, journal, bank debit and tax record may describe related aspects of one episode. Summing the screens can count the same event repeatedly.
3. **Dates have jobs.** Service date, invoice date, posting date, cash-settlement date, later edit date, and the date a participant could know something need separate fields.
4. **The endpoint can be unknown.** A bank batch can be observed while its final recipient is unclosed. Unknown is neither zero nor stolen nor legitimate.
5. **An employee is not a row called Employee.** Nicole Brunner's surviving imported label does not establish her presence or later actions (p.36).
6. **An account label is not a physical user.** Import Administration is not a character. Named platform activity needs distinct attribution from authenticated human action (pp.35–39, 47).
7. **The $141.578m and $141.749m fields overlap.** Different period windows and different measurement bases are explicit. Their near agreement cannot animate a dollar-by-dollar trail or become additive damages (pp.8–9, 46–47).
8. **2024 modeled input is unavailable.** The computational zero used in the discharge model is not evidence of no business activity (p.9).
9. **A contract maximum is not revenue.** Fees, estimated outside costs, paid amounts and recognized income are separate. Components already included in a budget are not added again (p.56).
10. **The appendix's endpoint grades are not a guilt scale.** They describe capability, records, intermediate cash and increasingly specific endpoints; entitlement and purpose still matter (p.47).

## Claim record required before historical content ships

Each claim needs an ID; exact proposition; source hash; document/page or native row/object locator; quoted/paraphrased status; currency/unit; amount precision; period; economic episode ID; population/grain; event date; record date; record-availability date, documented receipt/read date, and any separately labelled inference about knowledge (each may be unknown); endpoint; current proof boundary; supporting and conflicting sources; superseded-by link; and human review status. A mechanic adds learning objective, player verb, fabricated parameters, and failure cases.

Store the source's assessment separately from our review status. “Source calls this record-closed / exhibit not yet inspected” is a coherent state. “Verified” without saying what was checked is not.

The first five acquisitions are: current release manifest and corrections package; Creative Peaks/Reddit plus legitimate pooled controls; 2018 labor/access correspondence; contract/job/client lineage for one complete production; and the current object/settlement data underlying any aggregate view. The ERC workbooks, office/site records, “no yappin” message and tax/benefits details follow their prospective scenes. They should not hold up synthetic tests of basic gameplay.

## Addendum — Plate 04 war room (full overhaul)

*Five chairs at the table: financial auditor, data-vis analyst, strategist, designer, developer. Verbatim conclusions.*

**Auditor — what §2.2 establishes, ranked:** (1) $136,202,381 unattributable, corroborated two independent ways at 0.4%; (2) eight *named* rails, 1,868 entries, each mapped to a mechanism; (3) the temporal signature — two compositions, one aggregate: 2018–23 runs JustWorks+AMEX+Tricor (86%), 2024 runs EXTENSIS+Divvy+AMEX-tail, totals barely move; (4) the flip pins to two documented events. Any design must deliver them in that order.

**Strategist — the plate's one job:** a cold reader must grasp in ten seconds that *the total stayed flat while the parts swapped*. Plate 04 is Act II's output manifold; its takeaway is continuity-of-purpose across changing infrastructure. Everything else is reference layer.

**Analyst — form selection:** stacked bars hide lifecycles (failed v1). Gauge-tracks showed lifecycles but lost the flat-total story and read fussy (failed v2). The story "constant thickness, swapping bands" is the canonical case for a **banded flow** — but smooth streamgraphs interpolate data we don't have. Resolution: **stepped bands** — exact yearly rectangles, zero horizontal gap, fixed per-rail stacking order, so bands read continuously while every edge remains true to the matrix. Stack order puts AMEX at the baseline (the only rail alive all seven years — the constant thread) and gives **EXTENSIS the same tone and slot as JustWorks**, so the 2024 substitution reads as the same band changing shells — the visual thesis itself.

**Designer — plate composition:** (a) open with the claim, not the chart: a stat strip ($136,202,381 · 1,868 entries · 8 rails · 0.4% corroboration) on paper before the dark band; (b) **one hero, not two** — the banded flow *terminates into the void*: seven years of outflow pouring into the DataHole aperture at the right edge, fuse events as thin verticals through the bands; the previous separate void figure and chart competed; (c) **kill the hover UX and the era toggle** — replace with an always-visible **rail ledger**: name · total · entries · a seven-dot active-years span · mechanism § · finding chip. Nothing important lives behind a pointer; (d) captions written like a human: "Band depth is the year's dollars through that rail. The column height barely moves while the bands swap." Never again "stroke width ∝ dollar magnitude."

**Developer — QA list:** remove orphaned JS (toggle, hover readout, RAILSPEC listeners) and markup; per-cell values verbatim from the §2.2 matrix (which stays, demoted, as DATA); credits drawn as below-baseline notches, never netted into bands; fidelity suite + structure + screenshots at two widths.

## Addendum II — the density critique (v14) · designer × UX pair

*References on the table: Vert variable specimen (type as image, grain-black, cropped display), Radio Robida schedule (whitespace as structure), receipt/dot-matrix artifacts (document grammar as identity). Brief: air, nuance, tactility in choice moments, and the world-building note — every player, document type, and diagram is its own micro-brand.*

**R1 — Boxes are the density.** Nearly everything is bordered: 12 mechanism cards, 8 cast cards, perimeter cards, trigger cards. The references box almost nothing. **Ruling: only documents are objects.** Exhibits keep their borders and shadows — that becomes *semantic* (if it's boxed, it's a document). Everything else de-boxes to top-rules + whitespace; grids become lists with 64px gutters.

**R2 — Plate openings don't breathe.** Title → slash → kicker → content with no pause. **Ruling:** display scale up; 64px of air under the slash; ghost numerals enlarged and allowed to crop off the right edge (the Vert move); cover masthead up to ~190px with right-bleed crop.

**R3 — Facsimiles all wear the same dress → evidence reads as wallpaper.** The world-building fix: **each document *type* gets its own identity** under the shared № sticker system — email (soft corners, open header, double hairline), bank paper (square corners, tracked caps, tabular), tax form (square, double-rule outline), ledger (ruled paper), custodial report (dotted leaders), CashPro (an actual dark terminal panel), file listing (tree mono). The dossier's exhibit chrome stays constant — like evidence stickers applied to foreign papers. Same logic as a brand system: one program, many artifacts.

**R4 — The cast have no faces.** Players get **monogram marks** — geometric two-letter chips, with state encoded: principals filled (DE, DT), professionals outlined (MB, MG, CW), the conduit dotted (BM), **Sojka dashed (CS) — the excluded ring**. Identity grammar, not decoration.

**R5 — Grain is a wash, not a material.** Global 5% noise reads as dirty paper. **Ruling:** paper tooth drops to a whisper; **pixel-sharp grain moves to choice moments only** — the dark bands, dividers, closing, and terminal screen (the Vert black), via a fine high-frequency tile, screen-blended.

**R6 — Timeline is metronomic.** Every event the same size → no hierarchy. **Ruling:** five hinge events set larger and bolder (the pipe 5/20/2015 · activation 12/31/2019 · "Pretty much" 1/15/2024 · Amendment 4 11/16/2023 · buyout 10/3/2024); the rest stay quiet; rows gain air.

**R7 — Measures too wide for the new air.** Kickers to 56ch; card prose narrowed; mono captions stay full-width where they serve as rules.

**QA protocol:** two rounds (1440 / 390), structure parse + JS + fidelity after each batch; collisions fixed before presenting; no new figures invented (identity = dress, not content).

game structure

purpose and audience determine the intended experience. format provides the medium. role and agency define the available actions. session outcomes define completion.

purpose

the intended effect of the game beyond completing a session.

open what should the game help people understand, experience or do?

audience

the people the game is designed for, including their knowledge and context of play.

open who is playing, and what do they already know?

format

the medium and session structure through which play happens.

open which activities need a simulation, a reconstruction interface or a tabletop format?

player role

the position from which the player receives information and takes action.

open is the player an operator, an investigator or a participant in another defined role?

agency

the information, permissions and outcomes the player can change.

open which events remain fixed, and which respond to player decisions?

session outcome

the conditions that end play and the result presented at closeout.

open what constitutes completion, success or failure for the chosen concept?
